Cloud Model Browser

Tone3000 Integration — Thousands of NAM Models & IRs

Browse and download thousands of amp captures and cabinet IRs directly from within the app. The rich detail browser shows cover artwork, contributor info, and download stats — sort by Trending, Newest, Oldest, or Most Downloaded. Pick exactly the file variants you want (different cabs, mics, rooms) and they land in your library with cover thumbnails ready to scan.

MIDI Control That Matches How You Think

Four Mapping Tiers — Slot, Effect, Parameter, Action

Map your footswitch to a slot position, to a specific effect (RAT bypass = same CC across every preset), to a parameter knob, or to global actions like Tap Tempo and Preset Up/Down. Tap any slot in the live Active Preset view to learn a new mapping inline — no hunting through menus. CC chips show every mapping at a glance.
